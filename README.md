# Three Synth Problem

**An interactive musical instrument** built around an n-body gravitational simulation. Multiple different interaction modes are planned.

Currently in active development.

## The philosophy

When creating, we might experience Intellectual Blockers, but also Emotional Blockers. This idea has been explored in [Lu Wilson](https://www.todepond.com/)'s work, most notably [Arroost](https://www.todepond.com/report/arroost/).

The thesis is that the computer (through its tools) has the power to eliminate these blockers.

In Three Synth Problem, this idea is explored by letting the user relinquish part of the heavy burden of creation onto the Simulation. The synthesizer acts less like a "Tool" and much more like a semi-independent "Material". Like hot glass, watercolours, or ceramic : part of the joy of using it is collaborating with the material, a dialog made of light and heavy touch, laws of physics, actions and reactions, surprises. Playing rather than exerting our all-imperative will.

## Basic Use

Each Body in the Universe represents a different instrument. The Bodies move about by exerting their gravitational forces upon each other. Factors such as the position of each Body, its speed, or its relative distance to other Bodies, shape the sound and alter the parameters of the soundscape.
